1. Information We Collect

We may collect information in two main categories: information you provide voluntarily and information collected automatically through our games and website.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Customer Support & Inquiries: When you email us directly or submit inquiries through our contact points, we collect your name, email address, message contents, and any attachments or technical details you provide to resolve your question.
  • Feedback & Surveys: Any suggestions, comments, or bug reports submitted during open playtests or community communications.

B. Information Collected Automatically via Mobile Games

When you download and play our games, our analytics and advertising SDK partners may automatically process certain technical and device data, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / ADID), Android ID, Vendor ID, and random pseudonymized app installation tokens.
  • Device Hardware & Software Details: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, CPU architecture, screen resolution, system language, and carrier details.
  • Network & Location Information: IP address (used to infer general coarse location such as country or city level for localization and regulatory compliance; we do not collect precise GPS coordinates).
  • Gameplay & Telemetry Data: Game session start/stop times, level progression, scores, in-game achievements, virtual currency/item balances, crash logs, and frame-rate performance reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Game Functionality: To deliver gameplay, save progress, maintain game balance, and provide customer support.
  • Analytics & Improvement: To monitor crash logs, analyze retention, evaluate user experience, and optimize performance across devices.
  • In-App Advertising: To serve contextual or personalized advertisements through authorized ad networks, supporting free-to-play titles.
  • Fraud Prevention & Security: To detect malicious bots, abuse, unauthorized modification, and protect game integrity.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, including tax regulations, digital sellers specifications, and app marketplace developer policies.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Protecting the privacy of children is of paramount importance to Qaymaq Games. Our games are designed for general audiences unless explicitly marked otherwise on the store listing.

  • We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 where required by EEA/UK law).
  • If an application is participating in the Google Play Designed for Families program or directed toward younger audiences, our ad mediation SDKs are configured to request non-personalized, family-safe advertisements only.
  • If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete that data from our servers. Parents or guardians may contact us at support@qaymaqgames.com with any concerns.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Players)

Depending on your country or region of residence, you hold specific statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You have the right to request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of behavioral targeting at any time directly through your device settings:
    • Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
    • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

6. Mandatory Data Deletion Instructions (Google Play Requirement)

How to request deletion of your data:

Google Play policies require developers to provide a clear, public, and accessible mechanism for players to request deletion of their data. To delete your account or any gameplay data associated with your device, please follow these simple steps:

1
Send an email to our support team at support@qaymaqgames.com with the subject line: Data Deletion Request.
2
In your message, please state the name of the game you play and your pseudonymous identifier (such as your Google Advertising ID, found under Android Settings → Google → Ads, or your in-game User ID if displayed in the game settings menu).
3
Our team will review your request, purge associated player records from our active databases and attribution logs within 30 calendar days, and send you written confirmation once the deletion is finalized.

Please note: Certain transaction records (such as in-app purchase receipts processed by Google Play) may be retained where strictly necessary to satisfy statutory tax, accounting, or anti-fraud obligations.
