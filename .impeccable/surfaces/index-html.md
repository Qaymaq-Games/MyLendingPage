# Surface: Qaymaq Games studio landing (index)

Scope: full replacement of the studio home page (index.html). Prototype as a standalone artifact first, then port to the repo. Mode: Persuade (with Experience moments). Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine).

Audience and job: players and business contacts; learn the three games and what is coming, write to the studio, remember the studio. EN/RU switch. Legal links and app-ads.txt stay reachable.

Proof/content on hand: logo animation video, splash key art (low-res, cut into plates), Knight & Hamster screenshots/icon/feature art, Doom's Day cartridge art. No ratings, downloads, testimonials, team or founding facts: do not invent.

Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework; reduced-motion calm version; intro skippable; contacts readable as plain text.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a sumi-e ink scroll the visitor cuts open with the cat's katana; the brand intro is the door, the landing is a living ink painting, and every section is another sheet of the same paper. It refuses the category default of a dark game-studio grid of cover cards with neon accents.

OWN-WORLD: Rice-paper ground with paper grain, sumi ink blacks in washes from soot to mist grey, one vermilion (shu) red used for the sun, seals, slashes and the call to act; a night-ink band (navy-black, from the logo video) for the games. Heavy condensed display type from the wordmark, a calm Cyrillic-ready grotesque for reading, brush kanji only inside red hanko seals. Components are brush strokes, seals, hanging scrolls, torn paper edges and slash marks, never rounded cards.

STORY: Door of ink opens with the logo ritual and a slash; the visitor sees the studio's world, then its games (one released, one in the forge, one sealed with a hanko they can try to break), plays a short katana game with the cat, reads how the studio works and leaves with the contact address in hand.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed ink landscape (pine top-left, cat on cliff left, pagoda and banner right, red brush sun top-right painting itself in, mountain range and water mid-bottom, mist drifting, red leaves falling and scattering from the cursor). Center: the animated cat mark with red swirl over QAY MAQ, "Indie games for mobile & PC" below; the 猫 seal with a vertical rule left of the mark; vertical tagline SMALL IDEAS BIG WORLDS right of it. Thin top bar: seal-mark home, nav (Games, Play, Studio, Contact), EN/RU toggle. Primary action "See the games" under the subline, secondary "Write to us" in the nav.

FORM: Pinned by the user (splash key art + logo animation video), so no concept roll; position 1 of 1 on the list. Seed key: none (brief-pinned direction). Signature interaction: the katana slash, used three times as one motion grammar: the intro gate splits along the diagonal, the secret project's seal breaks on a held slash, and the mini-game is slashing falling leaves with a brush-ink trail.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
