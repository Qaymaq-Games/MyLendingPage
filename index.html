Qaymaq makes fine indie games

We build striking, readable and memorable game concepts with a handmade spirit. Our projects can be cozy, chaotic, mysterious or funny, but they always start with a strong player fantasy.

Qaymaq Games focuses on stylized visuals, fast prototyping, friendly systems and the kind of worlds players want to talk about after the screen fades out.
