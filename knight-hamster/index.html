Core Features

Why Players Love The Knight and the Hamster

🃏

Tactical Combat Depth

Dozens of distinct combat cards: sweeping slashes, iron-clad shield stances, enchanted arrows, and poisonous blades. Forge unstoppable synergies on every single run.

🐹

Battle Hamster Companion

Your furry partner is a true brother-in-arms! He cheers on the knight, distracts menacing foes, casts protective shields, and saves you from fatal strikes.

🏰

Kingdom Meta-Progression

Spend earned gold and rations to rebuild your ruined village. Upgrade the blacksmith, ancient shrine, and tavern for permanent hero stat bonuses.

🛡️

Fair Play & Offline Freedom

Zero intrusive banner ads or forced interstitials. Rewarded ads only play when you choose them for bonus perks. Fully playable offline anytime, anywhere.
